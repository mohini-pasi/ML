# Statistics - Full Structure

## 1. What is Statistics?

Statistics is the science of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and
interpreting data to make decisions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Types of Statistics

### A. Descriptive Statistics (Understand Data)

#### 1. Measures of Central Tendency

-   Mean
-   Median
-   Mode

#### 2. Measures of Dispersion

-   Range
-   Variance
-   Standard Deviation

#### 3. Data Distribution

-   Normal Distribution
-   Skewness

#### 4. Outliers

-   Unusual values in data

#### 5. Data Visualization

-   Bar Chart
-   Pie Chart
-   Histogram
-   Box Plot

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### B. Inferential Statistics (Predict & Decide)

#### 1. Probability

-   Chance of events

#### 2. Sampling

-   Study small data to understand population

#### 3. Estimation

-   Confidence Interval

#### 4. Hypothesis Testing

-   Test assumptions

#### 5. Relationships & Models

-   Correlation
-   Regression

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Simple Summary

Statistics = - Descriptive Statistics (Understand data) - Inferential
Statistics (Predict & decide)
